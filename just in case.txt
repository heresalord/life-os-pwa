### What Was Built:

1. **Database Schema & Pipeline Migration** ([`20261005_phase_c_notification_pipeline.sql`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/supabase/migrations/20261005_phase_c_notification_pipeline.sql)):
   - **`notifications` Reconciliation:** Added `payload JSONB` and `read_at TIMESTAMPTZ` with a trigger (`trg_sync_notification_read`) that keeps boolean `read` and `read_at` timestamps in bidirectional sync.
   - **`notification_preferences` Table:** Granular per-user, per-event preferences for `in_app`, `email`, and `push` with RLS.
   - **`devices` Table:** Unified push device registry supporting `'ios'`, `'android'`, `'web'`, and `'pwa'` with unique token constraints and RLS.
   - **`notify(user_id, event_type, payload)` Dispatcher:** Database function that validates user preferences against catalog defaults and automatically writes in-app notifications.
   - **Domain Triggers:**
     - `project.member_joined` emitted when project invite status transitions to `'accepted'`.
     - `share.invited` emitted when new collaboration items are assigned to a recipient.
     - `finance.budget_warning` migrated to emit with structured spending & budget payload.

2. **Event Catalog & Client API** ([`notifications.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/lib/notifications.ts)):
   - Defined `EVENT_CATALOG` with default channels for all C2 events (`project.member_joined`, `share.invited`, `friend.request_received`, `friend.request_accepted`, `note.shared`, `book.recommended`, `finance.budget_warning`, `task.reminder`).
   - Helpers: `getMergedNotificationPreferences()`, `updateNotificationPreference()`, `registerDeviceToken()`, `unregisterDeviceToken()`, and `sendNotification()`.

3. **In-App Delivery & UI** ([`NotificationCenter.tsx`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/components/notifications/NotificationCenter.tsx), [`NotificationContext.tsx`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/contexts/NotificationContext.tsx)):
   - Added tailored icons, backgrounds, and styling badges for all C2 event types.
   - Updated `markAsRead` and `markAllAsRead` mutations to record `read_at` timestamps locally in Dexie and sync to Supabase.

4. **Multi-Platform Push Integration** ([`useCapacitorPush.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/hooks/useCapacitorPush.ts), [`pushNotifications.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/lib/pushNotifications.ts), [`send-push/index.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/supabase/functions/send-push/index.ts)):
   - Web/PWA and native Capacitor push registrations automatically sync to the `devices` table.
   - `send-push` Edge function queries both `devices` and legacy token tables, with automatic cleanup of deactivated/stale tokens (404/410/UNREGISTERED).

5. **Type Safety** ([`schema.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/db/schema.ts), [`database.ts`](file:///Users/durockkoumassi/Life%20OS/src/types/database.ts)):
